The People Behind This Site

A set of interactive visualisations for EERI 124 — Electrotechnique 1 at the North-West University. It exists because the methods it walks through are worth practising more often than a lecture has time for.

Acknowledgements

JH

Prof. Johann Holm

Teaching & Learning · The Methods

The step-by-step methods this site walks through are his. The order of the steps, the point at which a supernode or a supermesh is introduced, and the insistence that every line stays ordinary algebra a student can follow — all of it comes from his lectures, where the methods were taught and proved long before any of it was written into code. What the pages here try to reproduce is the experience of being walked through a circuit by someone who has done it a thousand times.

GB

Mr. Gerrit Buter

Design & Development

Designed and built the site — the circuit generators, the solver that works every technique step by step, and the interface around them. It started as a way to practise the course's methods on more circuits than a lecture has time for, and grew into something that generates them, solves them one move at a time, and hands them to LTspice when you want a simulator to agree.

About the Site

Course
EERI 124 — Electrotechnique 1, DC resistive-network analysis, North-West University.
What It Covers
Node-voltage (KCL), mesh-current (KVL) and equivalent resistance — including supernodes, supermeshes and all four dependent sources — plus two study-unit 3 deep dives: the Wheatstone bridge and the Δ-Y transformation.
Take It to LTspice
Any circuit saves as an LTspice schematic or netlist, so you can simulate it and check the answer the workbench derived.
How It's Built
Static HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript. No build step, no framework, no tracking — it runs straight off the filesystem, offline.

This is a study aid, not an official course resource: where it and the study guide disagree, the study guide wins. Found something wrong? It is worth reporting — the whole point is that the steps match what you are taught.
